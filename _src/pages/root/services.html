Relocation and Moving Services

Nine services, each quoted on its own scope. You can book one of them, or the whole chain from packing to unpacking — but you should not be paying for a bundle that includes work you do not need.

If you are moving a home, start with house shifting. If you are moving a workplace, start with office relocation. Everything else on this page is either a component of those two or a standalone job.

Household relocation

  • House shifting

    The full chain for a home move: room-by-room packing, furniture dismantling, loading, transport, unloading, reassembly and unpacking. Scoped for 1 BHK through to villas.

    House Shifting Services

  • Local shifting

    Same-city moves that finish in a day. Priced on crew hours, vehicle size and carry distance rather than tonnage.

    Local Shifting Services

  • Long distance moving

    Intercity relocation on dedicated or shared vehicles, with an inventory checked at both ends and a realistic transit window.

    Long Distance Moving

Commercial relocation

  • Office relocation

    Workstations, seating, server racks, network hardware, records and pantry equipment, sequenced around a cutover window so the business restarts on plan.

    Office Relocation Services

  • Warehouse services

    Racked warehouse space for stock, project material or office inventory, with goods received, recorded and released against documentation.

    Warehouse Services

  • Storage services

    Short or long-term household storage for a lease gap, an overseas posting or a home that is not ready yet.

    Storage Services

Vehicle transportation

  • Car transportation

    Cars move on a carrier rather than being driven, so no kilometres are added and no road risk is taken. A written condition report is made at pickup and matched at delivery.

    Car Transportation Services

  • Bike transportation

    Fuel drained, battery terminal disconnected where needed, mirrors and levers removed, frame wrapped and braced. Crated for long routes or high-value machines.

    Bike Transportation Services

Standalone services

Booked on their own when you are handling the rest of the move yourself.

  • Packing services

    Full packing, partial packing or fragile-only. Double-walled cartons, bubble wrap, stretch film, corrugated rolls and furniture blankets, with the material charged on what is used.

    Packing Services

  • Loading and unloading

    Labour-only. Useful when you have your own vehicle, have already packed, or need muscle for stair work and a heavy piece or two.

    Loading and Unloading Services
